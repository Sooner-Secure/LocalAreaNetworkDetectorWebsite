PRIVACY POLICY.

A straightforward policy for a straightforward app.

NO DATA COLLECTED

LAND does not collect any data.

LAND, the local network scanner for iPad and macOS by Sooner Secure, does not collect any data from users. No personal information, network scan data, analytics, or usage information is collected by Sooner Secure through the app.

LAND does not require an account and does not include advertising or tracking. Network scan results are for your use, and CSV export allows you to save your findings yourself.

Questions or support

If you have questions about LAND or its privacy policy, contact pat@soonersecure.org.
